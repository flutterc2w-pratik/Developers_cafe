Question 3 — Simplify the dataset

Some column names are technical and some fields are not required for this analysis.

Task:
Use Edit Columns → Multi-Column Editor.

Rename:

PULocationID → pickup_location_id
DOLocationID → dropoff_location_id
tpep_pickup_datetime → pickup_datetime
tpep_dropoff_datetime → dropoff_datetime

Remove any unnecessary columns that you do not plan to use in the remaining analysis.

Business question:
“Can we make the dataset easier for analysts and business users to understand?”

This mirrors the rename/drop-column activity in the lab guide.
